Topsoil Pattern Variations

Eight neighboring blocks are shown for each approach to reveal repetition.

A · Broken Sediment

Short, irregular fragments at varied heights. Keeps the layered geology without continuous stripes.

B · Mottled Earth

Mostly clumps, grains, and small pockets. The least directional and least obviously tiled.

C · Mixed Sediment

Some blocks have broken layers, some are mottled, and some have compacted diagonal fragments.

D · Broad Patches

Larger, low-contrast soil pockets with sparse grains. Calmer at normal gameplay scale.
